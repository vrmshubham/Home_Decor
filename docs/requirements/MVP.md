# MVP scope

## Included

- responsive storefront
- categories and product catalogue
- size, colour, material and pattern variants
- product search and filters
- cart
- guest checkout
- customer accounts and order history
- COD and online payment support
- PIN-code serviceability
- local Bhavnagar delivery and store pickup
- custom-curtain enquiry form
- Sandook gift-set products
- Django Admin catalogue management

## Later

- native mobile apps
- loyalty programme
- advanced recommendations
- multi-store inventory
- interior-designer portal
